Evaluation of IEEE Research Paper Formatting Using Document Analysis
Alice Researcher, Bob Analyst
Department of Computer Science, Example Institute
Abstract—This paper presents a prototype system for inspecting research paper formatting and producing an explainable feedback score for authors. The approach combines text-structure checks with style evidence when available.
Index Terms—IEEE, research paper, document analysis, formatting evaluator
## I. INTRODUCTION
Researchers commonly make avoidable mistakes in the presentation of academic papers. Formatting tools can help identify missing parts and inconsistent document structure [1].
## II. METHOD
The system finds title, abstract, keywords, headings, references and citations. When richer documents are available, it also checks page layout and font properties.
## III. RESULTS
A prototype checker reports issues and suggestions to improve document preparation. Plain text does not include font size, margin, page or column information, so it is only checked for structure.
