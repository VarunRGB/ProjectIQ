# Research on Human Activity Recognition

## Related Works
Multiple sensor-based approaches have been studied [1, 2, 4-6].
Narrative references include Kim et al. (2021), while other authors (Garcia and Miller, 2023) explored wearable devices.
See https://example.org/har-overview, https://example.org/dataset-page and [the benchmark](https://example.org/har-benchmark).

## Implementation
The prototype uses accelerometer and gyroscope readings.
