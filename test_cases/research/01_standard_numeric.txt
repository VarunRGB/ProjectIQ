TITLE: Smart Irrigation Using Soil Sensors

1. INTRODUCTION
This report studies low-cost irrigation control.

2. LITERATURE REVIEW
Prior work by Smith et al. (2022) describes sensor-driven irrigation [1].
A second study evaluates soil moisture prediction (Kumar and Rao, 2024) [2].
The discussion also links to https://example.org/irrigation-survey.

3. METHODOLOGY
The prototype uses a moisture sensor and microcontroller.
